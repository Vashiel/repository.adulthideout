# AdultHideout 1.0.22 "Atlas"

**Released August 30, 2026**

AdultHideout now spans hundreds of independent sources, personal libraries, continuous channels, downloads and skin integrations. Atlas is the release that makes that world easier to navigate, preserve and return to.

That is why this release is called **Atlas**.

## Your AdultHideout, Preserved

Complete Backup & Restore can now carry the personal parts of AdultHideout to another installation: Vault, Website Collections, search history, custom presets, Continue Watching and safe preferences. Credentials, device-specific paths, caches and active download jobs remain deliberately excluded.

## Continue Where You Left Off

The optional Continue Watching history remembers the source and resume position of recently played videos. Finished entries clean themselves up, individual items can be removed, and the entire history stays local and can be disabled or cleared at any time.

## Live Favorites That Stay Useful

Chaturbate and Stripchat favorites now live inside AdultHideout without requiring a platform account. Models can be viewed as Online, Offline, Recently Seen or All, with fresh status checks and direct removal controls.

## Public PornHub Playlists

Atlas opens PornHub's public playlist catalog as normal Kodi folders, including pagination and continuous playback across playlist pages. No PornHub account is required for public entries.

## Search And Streams With More Range

Global Search gains Mega Shuffle across every enabled source plus finer presets for popular categories and specialist content. Smart Streams create a fresh randomized session on every explicit start, while the Full Movies channel continues to enforce a verified runtime of at least 70 minutes.

Confirmed uploader, channel and profile links can now open **More from this uploader** directly on supported websites. This remains intentionally separate from Explore Similar, which searches by metadata rather than exact uploader identity.

## A Better Kodi Interface

Website browsing again exposes List, Poster, Wide List, Wall and Fanart views. The selected view is reapplied after Kodi restores path-specific state, preventing Wall and other choices from unexpectedly falling back after navigation or playback.

LeakGallery gains video-safe pagination and richer discovery paths. Balbums filters non-video files and handles direct, API and WMV playback without looping. Numerous listing, artwork, DNS, proxy and playback paths were repaired across the catalog.

## Eleven New Websites

Atlas adds **AsianViralHub, ShowCamRips, 123AV, PornAVHD, ThotHub, InternetChicks, MyClassicP, RetroPornArchives, RetroPornGallery, VintageHunterPorn and VintageWorldP**.

Five currently unavailable sources are hidden by default rather than presented as broken folders. Their modules remain installed and can be restored through **Settings > Website options > Manage hidden websites** for later testing.

## Diagnostics That Distinguishes A Fault From An Outage

AdultHideout Diagnostics now retries temporary DNS and gateway failures when validating artwork and separates confirmed external outages from actionable addon failures. The final Atlas validation runs inside Kodi against the complete 287-source catalog.
