# AdultHideout 1.0.21 "Watchtower"

**Released August 24, 2026**

AdultHideout has become too large to understand one website at a time. Watchtower is the release that looks across the whole system.

Star Finder surveys thousands of performer profiles and their known film relationships. Smart Streams continuously watch and refill personal channels. Diagnostics and a broad reliability pass help keep watch over hundreds of independent sources whose APIs, protection layers and players can change without warning.

That is why this release is called **Watchtower**.

## Introducing Star Finder (Beta)

Star Finder turns a name into a path through AdultHideout.

Its compact local StarDB contains **25,000 performer profiles**, searchable aliases, detailed attributes and links to known full-length movie titles. Portraits remain remote, keeping the addon package practical while still giving compatible Kodi views rich artwork.

The new Filter Matrix can combine gender, ethnicity, height, cup size, hair, eye color, build and career era. Open a profile, discover known movies, send a title into the strict full-movie search or combine several performers into a Smart Stream.

Star Finder is marked **Beta** because metadata and film relationships come from imperfect external catalogs. The feature is already useful, but its coverage and matching will continue to improve.

## Introducing Smart Streams (Beta)

Smart Streams build continuous channels from AdultHideout instead of leaving playback at the mercy of a single website.

Start an instant 24/7 mix, choose a dedicated content channel, build a custom stream or combine several performers. AdultHideout refills the playlist in the background, avoids entries still waiting in the active queue and skips streams that fail before Kodi can begin playback.

The Full Movies channel and performer movie searches use a strict verified runtime of at least **70 minutes**. Short clips and entries without trustworthy runtime data are rejected instead of padding a movie channel with previews.

Smart Streams remain **Beta** because continuous playback crosses many unrelated websites, CDNs and resolver paths. Watchtower makes the system substantially more resilient while leaving room for device-specific refinement.

## Vault Becomes A Channel

Vault now supports personal **1–10 ratings**, visible star badges and sorting by rating, date or title.

Vault TV turns those choices into a continuous personal channel, prioritizing the highest-rated saved videos and repeating the collection when requested. Vault is no longer only where discoveries wait; it can now become something you watch.

## Better Full-Movie Discovery

Watchtower adds FilmAdult and StreamPorn, expands movie-focused searches across more existing sources and repairs PandaMovies, PornHD3X and MilfFox search paths.

Known performer filmographies can pass a selected title directly into AdultHideout's verified 70+ minute search. Unavailable FreeOMovie mirrors are rejected before they become dead results, and cross-site capture now tolerates sources that omit optional artwork arguments.

## Built For Kodi Skins

Star Finder, Smart Streams, their individual channels, Vault and direct search remain normal Kodi plugin folders and URLs. Compatible skins can place them on custom home screens without a second integration layer.

Direct skin-search results now expose real video thumbnails as poster, landscape and search fanart. Arctic Fuse 3 and similar skins can therefore show the selected result's actual artwork instead of repeating the AdultHideout background.

Per-site skin searches also receive a clean `direct_site_search` route that returns playable videos without Search, Categories or pagination folders leaking into the results.

## A Large Reliability Pass

Watchtower restores or strengthens difficult paths across the catalog:

- CumLouder listings after a metadata API mismatch
- FamilyPornHD and Milfzr listings after upstream layout changes
- Hypnotube's public age-gate session flow
- LeakGallery's embedded public-media parser
- WatchXXXFree's first-party antibot session flow
- XNXX content feeds after its former Gay and Trans directory routes changed
- XXVideoSS Cloudscraper listings
- Deflr CDN thumbnails and streams with expired certificates
- PervClips and SpankBang availability
- PandaMovies, PornHD3X and MilfFox movie searches
- Eporner and other proxy playback affected by initial-byte corruption
- dead-stream fallback and Smart Stream duplicate prevention
- deterministic Global Search relevance and reliable one-shot refresh
- Website Collections management and immediate Vault folder deletion

Diagnostics now samples up to three real video thumbnails and retries one completely empty Kodi directory response before declaring a website broken, reducing false failures caused by stale upstream cards or a transient first-load response.

## Four New Websites

**FilmAdult, StreamPorn, HDShemalez and VintagePornFun** join the catalog in Watchtower.

SwingerPornFun and VintagePornFun currently receive managed Cloudflare challenges that Kodi cannot complete reliably. Watchtower therefore hides both by default, while advanced users can still restore them through **Settings > Website options > Manage hidden websites** when availability changes.

Discontinued sources are removed instead of remaining as empty menu entries: Motherless, Rule34World, GoPorn and CollectionOfBestPorn are no longer included.

## Smaller Without Giving Up The New Features

The StarDB and package layout were rebuilt around compact local indexes and on-demand compressed movie shards. The former 12.71 MB SQLite database and duplicate performer data were replaced while retaining 25,000 profiles, 70,370 movie records, 214,560 relationships and curated website metadata.

Together with removed duplicate artwork and development leftovers, the final clean addon tree fell from approximately **23.18 MB to 12.37 MB**, a reduction of about **47%**, despite the new discovery and streaming features.

## Before You Begin

Star Finder and Smart Streams are clearly marked **Beta** in the Kodi menu. They are optional additions to the stable Global Search, Vault, Download Center, Offline videos and website browsing foundation.

Update through the AdultHideout Repository, restart Kodi once after installation and use **Settings → General → AdultHideout Diagnostics** when a source behaves differently on a particular device.
