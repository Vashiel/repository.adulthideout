# AdultHideout 1.0.28 "Lighthouse"

**Released November 18, 2026**

Lighthouse brings 14 new websites, length and quality filters for Global Search, page fetches through AdultHideout Core and a new diagnostics checklist that tested every website and uncovered problems on more than twenty of them, all fixed in this release.

---

### 14 New Websites

Anybunny, CamHub, CamWhoresBay, Erogarga, Extreme.Adult, FullTaboo, Incesto69, PervTube, PlayVids, TabooDude, TabooJizz, TabooTube, WatchPornX and WildKittys. Every one lists videos, searches and plays, and each comes with its own original logo.

---

### Global Search

- **Length & Quality Filters:** Two new entries in the Global Search menu. Length: any, 10+, 20+, 40+ minutes or full movie. Quality: any, 720p and better, 1080p and better, 4K only. The choice stays until it is changed.
- **Filters at the Source:** eporner, SpankBang, XNXX, xHamster and TubePornClassic apply the filters on their own side, so every page holds only matching videos. The Core drops videos that are too short while it parses.
- **Word Matching:** Multi-word queries match by words in any order ("asmr hospital" finds "Hospital ASMR ...").
- **More Sources in the Core:** Site profiles let 57 more sources search directly in AdultHideout Core, 281 in total. 66 sources whose pages need their module keep searching in Python, with 16 workers and 15 seconds per site.
- **Leaner Data:** Results are stored per search instead of in the shared state file, which had grown to 26 MB.

---

### AdultHideout Core 1.0.3

- **Site Profiles:** The Core runs AdultHideout's own search patterns for 57 more websites, so 281 sources now search natively, with at least 90% of the results of the dedicated module and about three times faster per site.
- **Failure Reasons:** The Core reports why a website failed (offline, not found, rate limited, Cloudflare), and Global Search skips retries that cannot succeed.
- **Honest Challenge Status:** An unsolved Cloudflare challenge is reported as an error instead of a success.
- **Diagnostics Support:** The Core reports the final address and page language of every website.
- **Fresh Build for All Platforms:** Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel), Android (64-bit & 32-bit) and Linux (`x86_64`, `aarch64` & `armv7`); installed Cores are replaced automatically on the next start.

---

### Core & Playback

- **Page Fetches through the Core:** Website pages, including modules built on urllib, are fetched through AdultHideout Core. Video, HLS and thumbnail transfers stay direct.
- **No More Stalls:** A failed resolve ends cleanly. While a video is still resolving, playlists no longer press Next and start one resolve after another.
- **32-bit ARM Linux:** 32-bit Kodi on CoreELEC, LibreELEC and Raspberry Pi gets the native `linux-armv7` Core.

---

### Diagnostics 0.3.0

The diagnostics add-on now runs a checklist for every website: reachable domain, English start page, buttons, listed videos, real thumbnails, a playable 5th video and Core use. It checks 16 websites at once and works in every player. Its known-issue list was re-tested completely: seven sites work again, two dead ones were removed, and only MyClassicP and VintageWorldP remain behind an interactive Cloudflare challenge.
