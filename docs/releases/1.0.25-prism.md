# AdultHideout 1.0.25 "Prism"

**Released October 17, 2026**

Prism focuses on playback precision, adaptive stream resolution, and an extensive diagnostic audit across the entire add-on catalog. It resolves forced 360p video playback on XVideos and XNXX, repairs and modernizes around 30 broken tube scrapers and video hoster resolvers, and strengthens parameter handling and diagnostic stream extraction.

That is why this release is called **Prism** — symbolizing optical clarity and the dynamic separation of stream manifests into their full resolution spectrum (from 360p up to 1080p Full HD).

## HLS Stream Quality Selection (Issue #78)

XVideos and XNXX serve their adaptive HLS master playlist with stream variants ordered as 480p, 1080p, and finally 360p. Previous versions flattened the master playlist by naively choosing the last variant, locking video playback permanently into 360p regardless of the configured quality setting.

Prism updates `HlsProxyController` in `proxy_utils.py` to:
- Comprehensively parse `#EXT-X-STREAM-INF` tags for `RESOLUTION`, `NAME`, bitrate, and stream URIs.
- Evaluate the user's preferred playback quality (`global_quality`) via `select_quality_variant()` (Highest available, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p).
- Prioritize adaptive HLS manifests over standard-definition direct MP4s (`mp4_sd.mp4`).

## Direct Stream Extraction (`resolve_recording_stream`)

Added `resolve_recording_stream()` support across XVideos, XNXX, and modernized scrapers. This allows downloads, recording features, and standalone diagnostic tools to retrieve stream URIs, headers, and format extensions directly without launching in-process HTTP proxies or player guards.

## Robustness & Parameter Ordering

Corrected parameter ordering across scrapers where list items previously inverted titles and URLs, and hardened integer conversion on add-on settings to avoid crashes when settings return non-numeric strings.
