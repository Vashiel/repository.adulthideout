# AdultHideout 1.0.24 "Rosetta"

**Released October 3, 2026**

Rosetta expands AdultHideout's international catalog, adds offline Cyrillic transliteration and title translation, integrates 19 new websites across German, retro, amateur casting, animation and international categories, and repairs playback across several key resolvers.

That is why this release is called **Rosetta**.

## 19 New Websites

Rosetta adds 19 new websites across diverse genres:

- **Animation & Cosplay**: SFM Compile introduces 3D animated source material with character categories, while Hentai Cosplay receives in-process HLS proxy routing for dependable playback.

## Offline Cyrillic Translation

AdultHideout now includes a local, offline transliteration and dictionary translation engine (`cyrillic_utils.py`). Titles, categories and performers from Cyrillic sources are transliterated into Latin characters and translated locally without requiring external online translation services or API keys.

## Resolvers & Playback Repairs

- **Veev Resolver**: Added full Veev resolver support with LZW decoding and automatic fallback for expired or non-video streams.
- **Hentai Cosplay**: Switched to in-process HLS proxy streaming with playback guards to eliminate mid-stream connection drops.
- **Source Updates**: Updated PandaMovies domain and listing parsing, AShemaleTube video card extraction, HotMovies pagination, XVideos stream extraction and SxyPrn domain fallbacks.

## Website Cleanup

- Removed **Pornobolt**, **VagoSex** and **PornoHimmel** after their video CDN endpoints became permanently unreachable.
